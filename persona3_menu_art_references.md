# Persona 3 Reload menu illustration references

The lookup distinguishes source scenery from terminal interface. The
game's floor selection is a list rather than a gallery of little interior
portraits. The portraits already on the terminal lift have been redesigned
as native drafting-space drawings; no game pixels or copied layouts are used.
Checkpoint navigation, marker numbers, and status copy retain their existing
layout. Upper and lower sub-plates use different framing of a shared block.

- [ATLUS's official game system and Tartarus screenshots](https://p3re.jp/en/)
  and [SEGA's original November 2023 Tartarus press materials](https://www.sega.jp/topics/detail/231109_4/)
  are publisher references for the exploration and reward-screen look.
- [Groudon0199's own game capture of all six blocks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fcwar9AubGI)
  indexes the environments at **2:15** (Thebel), **14:44 / 24:19** (Arqa),
  **35:33 / 51:25** (Yabbashah), **1:07:30 / 1:24:35** (Tziah),
  **1:42:36 / 1:59:06** (Harabah), **2:17:53** (Adamah). Reference stills show
  the [green windows and tiles at Thebel](https://img.playstationtrophies.org/images/monthly_2024_02/guide/persona-3-reload-tartarus-floors-3_90a5dd72-7589-4799-9509-5ed2d1685e4b_l.jpg),
  [Arqa's violet folds](https://img.playstationtrophies.org/images/monthly_2024_02/guide/persona-3-reload-tartarus-floors-4_f9c4ac0b-6995-4912-b39c-8760dfc34f0d_l.jpg),
  [industrial Yabbashah](https://img.playstationtrophies.org/images/monthly_2024_02/guide/persona-3-reload-tartarus-floors-5_9ca30f89-3243-4b91-a671-de3e2b874e06_l.jpg),
  [gilded Tziah](https://megatenwiki.com/images/3/3d/P3R_Tziah_Block_Screenshot.png),
  [Harabah's shifted checkerboard](https://img.playstationtrophies.org/images/monthly_2024_02/guide/persona-3-reload-tartarus-floors-7_c2bc3bd0-a5d8-4012-9ebe-236eabb74703_l.jpg),
  and [Adamah's optical rings and sphere](https://img.playstationtrophies.org/images/monthly_2024_02/guide/persona-3-reload-tartarus-floors-9_5185fcbe-6594-490b-ae37-72e49e76a27f_l.jpg).
- Shuffle Time's [ATLUS press screenshot of the reward-card interface](https://images.rpgsite.net/image/da49c9a1/131332/original/Persona-3-Reload_20231109_02.png)
  informed silver tracery and selected-card highlights; an [original in-game
  screenshot of Coins and Cups](https://images.gamebanana.com/img/ss/requests/65dbac448917d.jpg)
  shows their jewel and cup silhouettes. The terminal's generic Persona mask
  is intentional and does not substitute a portrait of one particular Persona.
- The SEES board's moon is not a reproduction of a Reload screen. Its durable
  event badge now depicts a cyan rim, uneven shading and surface craters,
  inspired by the [green-blue nocturnal language in ATLUS's official Dark Hour art](https://p3re.jp/en/).
  The board badge appears only in the same existing full-moon slot; the other
  event indicator and noticeboard are unmodified.

References were inspected as publisher/source stills, not by analyzing a
complete playthrough of the linked recording. All shapes, their cropping and
dither are original menu illustrations drawn on the shared 2×4-dot canvas.
