# Persona 5 Royal: Showtime source notes

The eight attacks keep their existing terminal names, damage behavior, and durations. The imagery is new, drawn from vector shapes onto a two-column by four-row dot grid for every terminal cell. No screenshots, video, dialogue, symbols traced from an image, or game assets are played or shipped. The gallery and live combat resolve the same clips.

The primary visual sources below are recorded stills of the game, published by the person playing it. Two additional recorded gameplay compilations provide alternate versions: [all Royal Showtimes (including alternates)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NzZLv8X2sJA) and [normal, finishing, and boss takes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F6BYdZ34KOM). The frame comparisons below were made from the linked rendered still sequences. The video pages provide secondary cross-checks; they were not played through in this environment. Staging is adapted, not an exact frame-by-frame reconstruction, and alternate battle outcomes share a single cinematic.
