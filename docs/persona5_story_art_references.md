# Persona 5 field interactions: art/reference audit

These are original illustrations on the terminal's 2×4 dot canvas. Published gameplay captures and extracted retail-game scripts establish the source motifs below. The exact framing, hardware geometry and UI layout are new. No screenshots, proprietary sprites, video, or added dialogue are bundled.

| Live illustrated fixture | Game evidence | Terminal adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spaceport hydraulic press | [Royal field script: the team uses the factory press control](https://mysh.dev/tools/persona/5r/view?file=%5CdataR.cpk%5Cscript%5Cfield%5Cfscr0156_009_010.bf); [production-line gameplay still](https://jusgameguide.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/screen-shot-2019-06-16-at-4.40.21-pm-e1560674474348.png) | Original cutaway with blue-green metal columns, raised/lowered piston and receding catwalk. The state is readable by the actual physical gap, not just lamp color. It does not imply a running countdown after the terminal's safe choice. The five-second retry, thirty-second opening, eight-second requirement and existing selection behavior are unchanged. |
| Casino Giant Slot | [Royal field script: inspecting the giant slot and its terminal](https://mysh.dev/tools/persona/5r/view?file=%5CdataR.cpk%5Cscript%5Cfield%5Cfscr0157_010_130.bf); [Royal gameplay capture, Slot Room section](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x5LDcdELNwo&t=3582s) | Original compact cabinet with separate reel collars, amber trim and two diagnostic lamps. The lamps show only the panel state already found by the player; their placement on the cabinet is diagrammatic, not a navigational claim about the game's separate terminals. Existing exact 777 and coin readouts stay legible and still appear only when the actual game state permits. |
| Laboratory Twilight Corridor switch | [Royal gameplay route recording](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1gV411o7em/); [captured Twilight Circuit route still](https://samurai-gamers.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/sg-p5r-maruki-palace-blue-grief-seed-location.jpg) | Compass-neutral threshold sketch: red lit span to a visibly barred ward, or blue lit span to the opposite portal. The inaccessible side has a physical gap as well as muted color. There are more color combinations in the Royal game; this illustration represents only the terminal's established two-state room. Explicit Entry, Closed Ward and Eden labels remain text. |

**No existing sprite plate:** `first_escape.py`, `bank_vault.py`, main `campaign_story.py` beats, the Laboratory archive/consultation, and the other Casino interludes are descriptive text and/or functional controls. They were not replaced with decorative artwork. PIN digits and inspected clues are indicators, not decorative graphics. They keep their original text and reveal rules. For comparison: [Castle escape actual gameplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_dqTwrGvOZg) and [Kaneshiro’s actual Vault 3F gameplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=285Ye9Pz7GA).
