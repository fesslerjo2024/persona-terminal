# Persona 3 Reload: static story artwork references

Original terminal illustrations on a native 2 × 4 microdot grid. The source
pages, character profiles, and available gameplay descriptions/chapter
indexes were used as references. The rooms, camera angles, costume
simplifications, and lighting are drawn for this terminal, not extracted
frames, and the research is not a claim of full video playback. See
[reel references](persona3_animation_references.md) for the animated material.

| Live scene | Game reference | Terminal staging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iwatodai station, evening and first light | [ATLUS West: The Hero’s Arrival](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNGZiyvFkL8), [ATLUS Games: Facilities Showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZTY0T6Efvc) | Overhead station beams lead into two sweeping track pairs. The night plate uses green-blue lamps and a bag; first light uses tiny window lights, a cup and notebook tied to the terminal's scene rather than claiming those objects are in Reload. |
| The dorm roof and Orpheus | [ATLUS official character and story profiles](https://persona.atlus.com/p3r/index.html?lang=enbuy), [English Gameplay Reveal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HnZJL1Mg900) | Garden-rail lattice, aberrant moon, and Orpheus's collar and lyre are arranged as a single recognizable silhouette. |
| Iwatodai dorm, later dorm scenes, the radio relay | [ATLUS West: Iwatodai Dorm Life](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cn_QnQKAPzc) and [hero introduction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNGZiyvFkL8) | Warm communal room, angled desk, key and cup. The radio face, signal trace and antenna distinguish field coordination from ordinary dorm scenes. |
| Velvet antechamber story plate | [ATLUS West: Introducing the Velvet Room](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJ1XMdBEFN0) | Layered blue lift grille, clock, paired sconces and book, distinct from the full descent cinematic. |

| Champs de Fleurs hallway, room mirrors, reunion, and morning exit | [Recorded July Full Moon operation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PKBHa3rKK9I&t=273s) | Carpet and framed doors, paired arched entries, yellow-orange morning facade.
