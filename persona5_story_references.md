# Persona 5 Terminal: story reel staging references

These are new original drawings at two by four independently addressed microdots
per character cell. No screenshots or video frames are loaded at runtime. The
capture links identify the source scenes, not exact shots that the terminal
reproduces.

| Live reel | Primary source | What carries over and what was staged for the terminal |

Persona 5 Terminal's city fly-in, folding paper and menu landing are original staging rather than claims about the game's opening shots. |

Publicly indexed publisher descriptions, source capture listings and the
available in-game capture references were checked. Streaming video playback
and frame-by-frame comparison were not available in this verification
environment. The casino/castle escape sequences and the December Velvet
story remain playable story panels, not previous animations; no new cutscene
gates or changes to their narrative, timing, or save checkpoints were added.
