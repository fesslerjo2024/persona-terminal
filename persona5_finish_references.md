# Persona 5 Royal finishers and Oracle: visual references

The terminal art is original, drawn in vector-like microdot planes at the active viewport size. **Reference** below means what the game footage, directly captured stills, or publisher material show; **adaptation** describes our staging rather than a claim about the original. The game capture was available as indexed footage and published frames; this was not a frame-by-frame video review. No game sprites, screenshots, or video are shipped. Some linked footage contains late-game spoilers.

The collective attack uses the participating in-game cast, then resolves into either a finishing pose or a regrouping without a caption. That distinction, and the timing of the existing cue, remain owned by the battle.
