# Persona 3 dot reels: references and staging

These are original illustrations on a 2 × 4 terminal dot grid. No game
screenshots, sprites, character dialog, or video was embedded. The official
SEGA / ATLUS feature descriptions and character profiles were consulted, along
with public indexes and chapter summaries for first-hand gameplay recordings.
The individual motion paths below are our adaptation; this is not a claim that
each reference video was played or that the render is shot-for-shot.

## Ending and discovery plates (live only)

The Promised Day recording indexes the graduation scene, the waiting rooftop
and the credits. The alternate ending gameplay is a reference pointer for the
graduation/karaoke branch. The adapted **graduation** paints curtain pleats,
audience and lectern; **roof** and **reunion** use a moving spring skyline,
railing, petals and separate character silhouettes; **credits** follows a
rippled horizon and a dot-density fade. **Karaoke** uses silhouettes,
handheld microphones and a moving waveform before fading. **Stars** is the
terminal adaptation's night interstitial. **Bridge/classroom** shows windows
and school desks. **Station alley** shows rail perspective, wall lamps and
small figures, while **Tartarus roof** imports the high rooftop silhouette,
Aigis motif and aberrant moon. Those incidental background details and all
motion timing are original designs rather than exact game footage.
