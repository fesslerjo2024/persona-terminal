# Selector and Persona 3 title references

These are original terminal drawings, not captured video frames or a
transcription of an opening. Only the named references informed these designs.

| Reel | Reference used | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Persona 3 selector | [SEGA / ATLUS Reload overview](https://asia.sega.com/p3r/en/switch2/) identifies the midnight changeover. | Pocket clock with an engraved-style face and independently moving hands. The clock is a visual shorthand, not a particular shot from Reload. |
| Persona 3 title | [ATLUS Reload overview and art](https://p3re.jp/en/) describes the Dark Hour and shows Tartarus; [publisher's gameplay footage listing](https://www.gematsu.com/2024/01/persona-3-reload-gameplay-the-first-50-minutes) identifies the early shift as an appropriate reference. | A nocturnal waterfront changes color after midnight; a segmented Tartarus silhouette rises in front of the moon. The waterfront-to-clock composition and pacing are original; it is not a reproduction of the title music video. |
| Persona 4 selector | [ATLUS Persona 4 Golden overview and game images](https://persona.atlus.com/p4g/) identifies the Midnight Channel motif. | Gold CRT with swinging antennae, tuning knobs, and a rolling scanline. This is only the selector; Persona 4 remains unplayable. |
| Persona 5 selector | [ATLUS calling-card art](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2019/12/03/persona-5-royal-release-date-announced-special-editions-detailed/feed/) and [ATLUS calling-card hat motif](https://shopatlus.com/collections/persona-5-royal). | An off-axis calling card with red print and a white crest. A dagger drops in and pins the card; this miniature transition is original. |

The Spaceport header hatch is inspired by the color-coded pressure doors in [captured Royal gameplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8JVSTLyE0I). The gallery-only damage demonstrations use an original Jack-o’‑Lantern target, based on the in-game Jack-o’‑Lantern design; the stage and its wave cue are diagnostic illustrations, not in-game cutscenes.

The optional Persona 3 calendar band uses the moon from the Reload Dark Hour
motif. The Tartarus rescue notice now depicts a radio with a short waveform.
Neither art element claims to reproduce a game camera angle. It leaves the
briefing wall radar removed.
