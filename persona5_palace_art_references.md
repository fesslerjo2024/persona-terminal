# Persona 5 Terminal: Palace Map artwork references

The original game explains what kinds of places these are; the terminal maps are not floor-plan copies. Each environment and instrument is newly drawn on the 2×4 dot canvas. Ordinary text still carries operations and grades. Unavailable operations don't reveal names, room-specific art, or gated floor labels. Small wayfinding marks (Pyramid route/fragments and Casino dock) still show live state.

The map art references are recorded game captures or the game's publisher. No screenshots, game maps, extracted images, or video are used by the terminal app. Individual replay hosting did not consistently serve the original full-size images, so the visual survey used available indexed in-game capture previews and locations in the recorded playthrough; it did not rely on continuous video playback.

## Approved terminal layouts

The live map update builds on those visual references and the independently
rendered 65×25 and 96×35 concept previews. The concepts are original layout
studies, not screenshots of the original game:

- **Castle:** CA-01 stair-cutaway route with brighter connecting treads, and CA-05's layered portcullis for the entrance.
- **Museum:** MU-05 gallery perspective, with dark placeholders where Story locks an exhibit.
- **Bank:** BA-01 brushed steel vault with a bright needle and muted inactive detent numbers.
- **Pyramid:** PY-07 dune approach until the papyrus is recovered in Story; PY-01 chamber cutaway after that, and throughout Battle Practice to keep selection positions stable.
- **Spaceport:** SP-01 six workcells, with a connected conveyor loop showing the order between rows without changing the shared navigation keys.
- **Casino:** CS-01 brass lift with CS-03 roulette detail once High Limit is available, cleared away from operation names.
- **Cruiser:** CR-01 broadside with a brighter selected deck and the existing letter progress.
- **Depths:** DP-04 terminal platform and DP-05 fractured bridge on the unlocked late route, with a dotted guide between operations 03 and 04.
- **Laboratory:** LB-01 intake sheet with quiet rules behind the text; LB-03 optics reveal the light puzzle and highlight the beam when that operation is selected.

The chapter/brief UI, actual operation order, Story unlock logic, medals,
and save schema remain independent of the visual layouts. Later artwork is
conditioned on the available Story operation, not on a locked cursor alone.
